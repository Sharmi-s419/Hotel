Historic 9th-Century Temple

Thanumalayan (Sthanumalayan) Temple, Suchindram

A rare, majestic architectural wonder where the Hindu Trinity—Lord Shiva (Sthanu), Lord Vishnu (Mal), and Lord Brahma (Ayan)—are worshipped as a single deity. Renowned worldwide for its 134-foot Gopuram, towering 18-foot monolithic Hanuman statue, and ancient musical stone pillars.

Morning Darshan: 04:30 AM – 11:30 AM
Evening Darshan: 05:00 PM – 08:30 PM
Dress Code: Traditional Indian attire (Dhoti / Sarees)
Distance from Hotel: ~1 km (Approx. 3 mins drive / short walk)
